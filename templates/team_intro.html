Who We Are

Licensed engineers, local roots

Miller Engineering is a Morgantown-based MEP firm serving public and private clients across West Virginia, Pennsylvania, and Maryland. Since 2003 we've built a reputation for doing the work right the first time.

Different by Design

Engineering built on a detailed process

Owned and led by Craig Miller, PE, Miller Engineering approaches every project through investigation, observation, open communication with stakeholders, system analysis, and building modeling — with the entire team engaged from day one.

Over its history, MEI has engineered solutions for more than $23.2M in MEP system upgrades, repairs, and renovations — for clients ranging from private owners to local and state governments. A strict attention to detail has kept the firm's change-order rate under 0.1%, well below the industry norm.

Every member of the team brings hands-on mechanical experience — installation, construction, design, and maintenance — so the designs we deliver are built to be constructed and built to last.
